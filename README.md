## 📌 Overview
**Alice in Borderland** is a Japanese science fiction thriller drama series directed by *Sato Shinsuke* and based on the manga by *Haro Aso*. The story follows gamer Ryōhei Arisu and his friends, who find themselves suddenly trapped in a deserted, parallel version of Tokyo where they are forced to compete in deadly games to extend their "visas" and survive.

---

## 👥 Main Characters

* **Ryōhei Arisu** (*Kento Yamazaki*) - A lethargic gamer whose puzzle-solving and analytical skills shine in games of intellect.
* **Yuzuha Usagi** (*Tao Tsuchiya*) - A skilled mountaineer whose physical endurance and survival instincts complement Arisu.
* **Chishiya** (*Nijirō Murakami*) - A cunning, highly intelligent medical student who plays for his own survival.
* **Kuina** (*Aya Asahina*) - A former fashionista with exceptional martial arts and combat skills.

## 🗝️ Core Rules of the Borderland

1. **Visas:** Every player is issued a visa upon entering the Borderland; winning games extends the visa duration in days.
2. **Game Arena:** Players must enter enclosed arenas to participate in mandatory games.
3. **Failure to Clear:** Letting a visa expire results in execution by a laser descending from the sky.
